## Understanding Pediatric GERD

Pediatric GERD is a common condition where the stomach acid flows back into the esophagus, causing discomfort, pain, and difficulty feeding. In infants and children, this can lead to frequent spit-ups, vomiting, and refusal to feed. The monsoon season in Kochi, with its high humidity and changes in temperature, can exacerbate GERD symptoms, making it essential for parents to seek gentle and effective management approaches.

- Symptoms of Pediatric GERD include frequent spit-ups and vomiting, refusal to feed, and discomfort during feeding
- The monsoon season can worsen GERD symptoms due to changes in temperature and humidity

## Lifestyle Changes for Managing Pediatric GERD

In addition to homoeopathic remedies, lifestyle changes can play a significant role in managing Pediatric GERD. Parents can try feeding their child smaller, more frequent meals, burping them regularly, and keeping them upright after feeding. Avoiding spicy or fatty foods, which can trigger acid reflux, is also essential. By combining these lifestyle changes with homoeopathic approaches, parents can help their child feel more comfortable and reduce the frequency of GERD symptoms.
