## Understanding Seasonal Allergies in Kochi

Kochi's unique climate, with its high humidity and rainfall during the monsoon season, can exacerbate allergy symptoms. This is particularly true for individuals with pre-existing sensitivities to food additives and preservatives, which are commonly found in processed and packaged foods. As the monsoon season progresses, the increased moisture in the air can lead to a proliferation of mold and mildew, further aggravating allergy symptoms.

- Increased humidity and rainfall during the monsoon season can exacerbate allergy symptoms
- Pre-existing sensitivities to food additives and preservatives can be particularly problematic during this time
- Mold and mildew growth can further aggravate allergy symptoms
