## Understanding Dust Mite Allergies

Dust mites are tiny creatures that thrive in humid environments, making Kochi's homes an ideal breeding ground during the monsoon season. Their waste products become airborne, triggering allergic reactions in susceptible individuals. Symptoms can include sneezing, congestion, itchy eyes, and skin rashes. In severe cases, dust mite allergies can exacerbate conditions like asthma, requiring immediate medical attention.

## Common questions

**How can I reduce dust mites in my home?**

To reduce dust mites, keep your home clean and dry, use a dehumidifier, wash bedding in hot water weekly, and consider using allergen-proof bedding covers. Regular cleaning, especially of carpets and upholstered furniture, can also help reduce dust mite populations.

**Can homoeopathy help with severe allergy symptoms?**

While homoeopathy can be beneficial in alleviating symptoms of dust mite allergies, severe reactions require immediate medical attention. If you experience difficulty breathing, rapid heartbeat, or other severe symptoms, seek urgent care at the nearest hospital or medical facility.
