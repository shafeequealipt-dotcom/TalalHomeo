## Understanding UTIs in Children

Urinary tract infections in children can be caused by a variety of factors, including bacterial infections, poor hygiene, and anatomical issues. It's essential for parents to recognize the signs and symptoms of UTIs, such as fever, dysuria, and abdominal pain, and seek medical attention if they suspect their child is suffering from a UTI. In Kochi, where the monsoon season brings high humidity, it's crucial to take preventive measures to reduce the risk of UTIs in children.

- Maintain good hygiene practices, such as wiping from front to back and washing hands frequently
- Encourage children to drink plenty of water to help flush out bacteria
- Avoid bubble baths and fragrant soaps that can irritate the genital area
