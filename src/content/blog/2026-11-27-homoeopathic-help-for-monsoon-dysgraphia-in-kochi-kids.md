## Understanding Dysgraphia and Its Connection to Monsoon Season

Dysgraphia is a neurological disorder that affects an individual's ability to write and draw, often characterized by poor handwriting, difficulty with spelling, and trouble with composition. In children, dysgraphia can be particularly challenging, as it may impact their ability to complete homework assignments, take notes, and participate in creative activities. The monsoon season in Kochi, with its high humidity and frequent rainfall, can further complicate matters, as children may spend more time indoors, leading to a lack of physical activity and outdoor play, which are essential for developing hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills.

- Increased screen time during monsoon season can lead to a sedentary lifestyle, contributing to poor hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills
- Lack of outdoor play can result in reduced opportunities for physical activity, exacerbating dysgraphia symptoms
