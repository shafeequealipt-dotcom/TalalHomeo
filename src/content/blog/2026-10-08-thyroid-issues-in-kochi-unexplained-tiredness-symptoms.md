## Understanding Thyroid Issues

Thyroid problems occur when the thyroid gland doesn't produce the right amount of hormones, which are crucial for energy production and overall health. Hypothyroidism, where the gland doesn't produce enough hormones, is a common condition that can cause fatigue, weight gain, and dry skin. On the other hand, hyperthyroidism, where the gland produces too many hormones, can lead to symptoms like weight loss, anxiety, and rapid heartbeat.
