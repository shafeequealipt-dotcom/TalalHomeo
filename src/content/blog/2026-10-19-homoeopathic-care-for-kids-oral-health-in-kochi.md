## Understanding Monsoon-Related Oral Health Issues

The monsoon season in Kochi brings with it a unique set of challenges for children's oral health. The increased humidity and moisture in the air can lead to a higher risk of tooth decay and gingivitis, as bacteria thrive in these conditions. Furthermore, the rainy season often disrupts regular routines, including oral hygiene practices, making children more susceptible to oral health issues.

- Tooth decay: caused by the buildup of plaque and bacteria on teeth
- Gingivitis: inflammation of the gums, often a precursor to more serious gum diseases

## Tips for Parents to Promote Oral Health in Children During Monsoon

As a parent, there are several steps you can take to promote your child's oral health during the monsoon season. Encourage regular brushing and flossing, and supervise your child to ensure they are using proper techniques. Additionally, limit sugary snacks and drinks, and opt for healthy, balanced meals instead. If you notice any signs of oral health issues, such as tooth sensitivity or gum inflammation, seek advice from a qualified homoeopath or dentist.

**What are the symptoms of gingivitis in children, and when should I seek medical attention?**
