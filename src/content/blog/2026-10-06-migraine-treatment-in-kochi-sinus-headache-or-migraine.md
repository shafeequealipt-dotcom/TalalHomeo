## Understanding Sinus Headaches

Sinus headaches are often caused by inflammation or infection in the sinus cavities, which can be triggered by allergies, colds, or other respiratory issues. Symptoms of sinus headaches typically include pain and pressure in the face, particularly in the cheeks and forehead, as well as nasal congestion and discharge.

- Pain and pressure in the face
- Nasal congestion and discharge
- Often accompanied by fever and fatigue

## Understanding Migraines

Migraines, on the other hand, are a type of neurological disorder that can cause intense, throbbing pain, usually on one side of the head. Migraine symptoms can also include sensitivity to light and sound, nausea, and vomiting. In some cases, migraines can be preceded by an aura, which is a visual or sensory disturbance that warns of an impending migraine.

- Intense, throbbing pain on one side of the head
- Sensitivity to light and sound
- Nausea and vomiting

## Common questions

**How can I tell if I have a sinus headache or a migraine?**

If you're experiencing pain and pressure in the face, particularly in the cheeks and forehead, along with nasal congestion and discharge, it may be a sinus headache. However, if you have intense, throbbing pain on one side of the head, sensitivity to light and sound, and nausea, it could be a migraine. It's always best to consult a healthcare professional for an accurate diagnosis.

**When should I seek urgent care for a headache?**

If you experience a sudden, severe headache, confusion, weakness, or numbness, seek urgent medical attention. Additionally, if you have a fever over 102°F, stiff neck, or vomiting, you should seek immediate care. It's always better to err on the side of caution when it comes to your health.
