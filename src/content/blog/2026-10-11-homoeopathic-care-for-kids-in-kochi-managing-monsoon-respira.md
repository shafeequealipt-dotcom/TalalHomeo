## Tips for Parents to Help Manage Monsoon-Related Respiratory Issues in Children

There are several steps parents can take to help manage their child's respiratory issues during the monsoon season. Keeping the home clean and dust-free, avoiding exposure to pollution, and ensuring the child gets plenty of rest and hydration can all help reduce the severity of symptoms. Additionally, monitoring the child's condition closely and seeking medical attention if symptoms worsen can help prevent complications.

- Keep the home clean and dust-free
- Avoid exposure to pollution
- Ensure the child gets plenty of rest and hydration
