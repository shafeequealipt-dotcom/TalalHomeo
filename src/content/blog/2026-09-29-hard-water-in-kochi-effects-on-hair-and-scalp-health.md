## Understanding Hard Water and Its Effects

Hard water is water that contains high levels of minerals, such as calcium and magnesium. These minerals can cause a buildup on the hair and scalp, leading to dryness, flakiness, and irritation. In Kochi, the water hardness can vary depending on the source and treatment of the water. However, in general, hard water can be damaging to hair and scalp health, especially for people with sensitive skin or existing scalp conditions.

To mitigate the effects of hard water, it's essential to use gentle hair care products and consider using a water filter or purifier to reduce the mineral content.
