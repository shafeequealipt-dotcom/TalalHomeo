Furthermore, the monsoon season often brings an increased risk of infections, making it crucial to support children's immune systems through a balanced diet and appropriate homoeopathic care.

- Reduced outdoor activities and sunlight exposure
- Higher susceptibility to infections

## Dietary Tips for a Healthier Monsoon Season

A well-balanced diet plays a crucial role in supporting children's health during the monsoon season. Parents in Kochi can help boost their child's immunity by incorporating nutrient-rich foods, such as fruits, vegetables, and whole grains, into their diet. Additionally, ensuring adequate hydration and limiting sugary or processed foods can help support overall health and well-being.
