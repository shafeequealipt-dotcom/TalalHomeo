## Common questions

**How can I differentiate between a common cold and sinusitis?**

While both conditions share similar symptoms, such as congestion and cough, sinusitis is typically characterized by facial pain, headache, and a prolonged duration of symptoms. If you're unsure, it's best to consult a healthcare professional for an accurate diagnosis.

**Can homoeopathy completely eliminate allergies?**

Homoeopathy can help manage and alleviate symptoms of allergies, but it's essential to understand that it may not completely eliminate them. The goal of homoeopathic treatment is to reduce the frequency and severity of allergic reactions, improving the overall quality of life.

**When should I seek urgent care for sinusitis or allergies?**

If you experience severe symptoms such as difficulty breathing, severe headache, or facial pain that worsens over time, it's crucial to seek urgent medical care. Additionally, if you have a fever over 102°F (39°C), or if your symptoms worsen despite treatment, you should consult a healthcare professional immediately.
