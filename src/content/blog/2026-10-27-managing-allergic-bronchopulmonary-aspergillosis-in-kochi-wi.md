## Understanding Allergic Bronchopulmonary Aspergillosis (ABPA)

Allergic Bronchopulmonary Aspergillosis is a condition where the airways react to a fungus called Aspergillus, leading to inflammation and symptoms such as coughing, wheezing, and shortness of breath. In Kochi, the monsoon season can exacerbate this condition due to the high humidity and increased fungal spore count in the air.

Homoeopathic practitioners in Ernakulam and Fort Kochi take a holistic approach to understanding ABPA, considering not just the physical symptoms but also the patient's overall health, lifestyle, and environmental factors.

- Symptoms of ABPA may include coughing, wheezing, and shortness of breath
- Increased fungal spore count in the air during monsoon season can exacerbate symptoms
