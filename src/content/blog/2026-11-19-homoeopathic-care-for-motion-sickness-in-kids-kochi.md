## Understanding Motion Sickness in Children

Motion sickness occurs when there is a conflict between the senses, particularly the inner ear, eyes, and sensory nerves. In children, this can be triggered by various factors, including travel by car, boat, or plane. The symptoms can range from mild discomfort to severe nausea and vomiting. Recognizing the signs early can help in providing timely relief and comfort to the child.

- Watch for signs of pallor, sweating, and nausea
- Keep the child hydrated with frequent sips of water

## Tips for Parents During Monsoon Travel

During the monsoon season in Kochi, it's essential for parents to be prepared when traveling with children. Keeping the child occupied with games, books, or music can help distract them from the discomfort of motion sickness. Ensuring good ventilation and avoiding heavy meals before travel can also be beneficial. If the child experiences severe symptoms, such as persistent vomiting or difficulty breathing, it's crucial to seek immediate medical attention.

- Choose seats in the middle of the vehicle for less motion
- Avoid screens at least an hour before travel to reduce visual stimuli
