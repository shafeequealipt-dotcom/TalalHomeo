Homoeopathic practitioners in Ernakulam consider these environmental changes when assessing patients' conditions, aiming to provide relief from symptoms without ignoring the root causes.

- Increased pollen counts due to altered flora growth patterns
- Longer pollen seasons affecting allergy sufferers

## Common questions

**Can homoeopathy completely eliminate allergies?**

Homoeopathy aims to reduce the severity and frequency of allergic reactions, but it may not completely eliminate allergies. It's a holistic approach that seeks to balance the body's response to allergens, providing relief from symptoms.

**How does homoeopathy differ from conventional allergy treatments?**

Homoeopathy focuses on individualized treatment plans that consider the patient's unique health profile and environmental exposures, unlike conventional treatments that often follow a one-size-fits-all approach. This personalized method can lead to more effective and sustainable management of allergies.

**When should I seek urgent care for an allergic reaction?**

If you experience severe symptoms such as difficulty breathing, rapid heartbeat, or a drop in blood pressure, seek urgent medical care immediately. These could be signs of a serious allergic reaction that requires prompt attention.
