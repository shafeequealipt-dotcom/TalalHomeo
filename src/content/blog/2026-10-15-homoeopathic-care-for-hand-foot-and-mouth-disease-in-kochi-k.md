## Understanding Hand, Foot, and Mouth Disease

Hand, Foot, and Mouth Disease is characterized by sores in the mouth and a rash on the hands and feet. It's highly contagious and spreads through direct contact with an infected person's respiratory secretions, such as saliva, sputum, or mucus, or through contact with contaminated surfaces or feces. In Kochi, the monsoon season creates an ideal environment for the virus to spread, making it essential for parents to be vigilant and take preventive measures.

- Practice good hygiene, such as frequent handwashing with soap and water
- Avoid close contact with anyone who has HFMD
- Keep your child home from school or daycare if they show symptoms

While homoeopathy can help manage HFMD, prevention is always the best approach. Parents in Fort Kochi and across Ernakulam can take several steps to reduce the risk of their child contracting HFMD.
