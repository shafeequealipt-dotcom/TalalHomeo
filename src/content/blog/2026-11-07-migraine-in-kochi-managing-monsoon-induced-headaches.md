## Understanding Monsoon-Induced Migraines

The monsoon season in Kerala, especially in areas like Fort Kochi and Ernakulam, is characterized by significant fluctuations in barometric pressure. These changes can trigger migraine attacks in sensitive individuals. Homoeopathy offers a holistic approach to managing such weather-induced migraines by focusing on the individual's overall health and susceptibility to environmental factors.

- Changes in humidity and temperature can also play a role in triggering migraines
- Keeping a headache diary can help identify personal weather triggers

While homoeopathy can provide relief from migraine symptoms, it's essential for individuals experiencing frequent or severe migraines to consult with a healthcare professional.
