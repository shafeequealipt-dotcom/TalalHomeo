## Understanding Indoor Allergens and Triggers

Indoor allergens and triggers can be found in various forms, including dust mites, mold, pet dander, and pollen. These allergens can exacerbate respiratory issues, such as asthma, and trigger allergic reactions. In Kochi's humid climate, mold and dust mites thrive, making it essential to take proactive measures to reduce their presence in your home.

- Dust mites: found in bedding, carpets, and upholstered furniture
- Mold: grows in damp environments, such as bathrooms and kitchens
- Pet dander: shed by pets, especially during grooming

## Monsoon-Proofing Tips for Your Home

To reduce indoor allergens and triggers, consider the following monsoon-proofing tips for your home: use a dehumidifier to maintain a relative humidity of 30-50%, regularly clean and dust surfaces, and ensure good ventilation. Additionally, using a HEPA air purifier can help remove allergens and particles from the air, creating a healthier living environment.

## Common questions

**How can I reduce mold growth in my home during the monsoon season?**

To reduce mold growth, ensure good ventilation, use a dehumidifier, and regularly clean and dry surfaces, especially in areas prone to moisture such as bathrooms and kitchens. If you suspect a serious mold infestation, consider seeking professional help.

**Can homoeopathy help with severe allergic reactions?**

While homoeopathy can be beneficial in managing allergy symptoms, it is essential to seek immediate medical attention if you experience severe allergic reactions, such as anaphylaxis. Our homoeopaths can work in conjunction with conventional medical treatment to provide comprehensive care.

**How often should I clean my home to reduce indoor allergens?**

Regular cleaning is essential to reduce indoor allergens. Aim to dust and vacuum surfaces at least once a week, and consider deep cleaning high-traffic areas and surfaces that accumulate moisture more frequently.
