Recurrent tonsillitis in children is a common concern for parents in Kochi and across Kerala. ## Understanding Tonsillitis in Children

Tonsillitis is an inflammation of the tonsils, which are two oval-shaped pads of tissue at the back of the throat. It's usually caused by a viral or bacterial infection, and symptoms can include sore throat, fever, and difficulty swallowing. In many cases, tonsillitis is a self-limiting condition that can be managed with rest, hydration, and over-the-counter pain relievers. However, when tonsillitis becomes recurrent, it can be a sign of an underlying issue, such as an allergy or a weakened immune system.

- Symptoms of tonsillitis in children include sore throat, fever, and difficulty swallowing
- Tonsillitis can be caused by a viral or bacterial infection
- Recurrent tonsillitis can be a sign of an underlying issue, such as an allergy or a weakened immune system
