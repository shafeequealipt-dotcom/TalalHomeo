## Understanding Monsoon-Related Sleep Disturbances in Children

The monsoon season in Kerala can bring about a range of factors that disrupt children's sleep, including increased humidity, noise from heavy rainfall, and changes in temperature. These environmental changes can lead to difficulty falling asleep, frequent waking, and daytime fatigue. Additionally, the monsoon season can also trigger allergies and respiratory issues, further exacerbating sleep disturbances.

- Increased humidity can lead to respiratory issues like bronchitis and asthma
- Noise from heavy rainfall can disrupt sleep patterns
- Changes in temperature can cause discomfort and restlessness

## Tips for Promoting Healthy Sleep Habits in Children During the Monsoon Season

In addition to homoeopathic remedies, there are several steps parents can take to promote healthy sleep habits in their children during the monsoon season. Establishing a consistent bedtime routine, creating a sleep-conducive environment, and encouraging physical activity during the day can all help regulate sleep patterns.
