## Understanding Oral Thrush in Children

Oral thrush is a fungal infection caused by the overgrowth of Candida albicans, a type of yeast that normally lives in the mouth. In children, oral thrush can cause white patches on the tongue, inside the cheeks, and on the gums, which can be painful and make eating and drinking uncomfortable. The monsoon season in Kochi, with its high humidity and warm temperatures, creates an ideal environment for the growth of this fungus.

- White patches on the tongue, inside the cheeks, and on the gums
- Pain or discomfort while eating or drinking
- Difficulty swallowing

## Maintaining Good Oral Hygiene in Children

Good oral hygiene is essential in preventing and managing oral thrush in children. Parents can help by encouraging their children to brush their teeth regularly, especially after meals, and to rinse their mouth with water after brushing. Additionally, avoiding sugary foods and drinks, which can contribute to the growth of Candida, is crucial. In areas like Ernakulam, where the water quality may vary, using clean water for oral hygiene is vital.
