## The Monsoon Effect on Sleep and Migraines

The monsoon season in Kerala, particularly in areas like Fort Kochi and Ernakulam, is known for its heavy rainfall and significant changes in humidity and temperature. These environmental shifts can affect sleep quality and duration, leading to irregular sleep patterns. For individuals prone to migraines, such disruptions can trigger or exacerbate migraine episodes. The relationship between sleep disturbances and migraine is complex, with sleep acting as both a potential trigger and a relieving factor for migraine pain.

- Changes in sleep patterns can trigger migraine attacks
- Irregular sleep can exacerbate migraine symptoms
- Environmental factors like humidity and temperature play a role in sleep quality
