## Common questions

**How can I prevent monsoon-triggered allergies in Kochi?**

While it's difficult to completely avoid exposure to pollens, there are steps you can take to reduce your risk of allergic reactions. Keeping windows and doors closed during peak pollen hours, using air purifiers, and avoiding outdoor activities during heavy rainfall can help minimize exposure. Consult with a homoeopathic practitioner to develop a personalized prevention plan.

**Can homoeopathy help with severe allergy symptoms?**

Yes, homoeopathy can be an effective approach to managing severe allergy symptoms. However, if you experience difficulty breathing, rapid heartbeat, or other severe symptoms, seek immediate medical attention. Our homoeopathic practitioners can work in conjunction with conventional medical treatment to provide comprehensive care.

**How long does it take to see results from homoeopathic allergy treatment?**

The time it takes to see results from homoeopathic allergy treatment can vary depending on the individual and the severity of their symptoms. Some patients may experience relief within a few days, while others may take several weeks to notice significant improvements. Be patient, and work closely with your homoeopathic practitioner to adjust your treatment plan as needed.
