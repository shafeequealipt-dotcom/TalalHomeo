## Understanding the Underlying Causes of Nose Picking in Children

Nose picking, or rhinotillexomania, is a common habit among children, especially during the monsoon season when the air is filled with allergens and irritants. In Fort Kochi and other parts of Kerala, the high humidity and presence of mold can exacerbate nasal congestion and discomfort, leading to increased nose picking. Homoeopaths consider the physical and emotional factors that contribute to this behavior, including allergies, nasal congestion, and anxiety or stress.

- Allergies to dust, mold, or pollen
- Nasal congestion due to colds or sinusitis
- Anxiety or stress related to school or social pressures
